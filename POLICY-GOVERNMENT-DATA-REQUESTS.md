# Government Data Request Policy — Jaggars Software Holdings LLC

**Effective date:** September 4, 2026 · **Owner:** Brice Jaggars, Founder
**Scope:** All products operated by Jaggars Software Holdings LLC ("JSH"), a Florida
limited liability company, including MyRealtyVault (myrealtyvault.com), and all
personal data JSH controls on its platforms. JSH acts as data controller; Supabase
Inc. and Netlify Inc. (IT solutions providers, United States) act as data processors
under contractual data-processing terms.

## 1. Policy statement

JSH discloses user data to a government authority only when required by valid legal
process, and then only the minimum data the process lawfully compels. JSH does not
provide any government authority with direct, standing, or bulk access to its
systems or user data.

## 2. What we require before disclosing

A request must arrive as valid legal process — a subpoena, court order, search
warrant, or the equivalent under the law of a jurisdiction with authority over JSH —
issued to Jaggars Software Holdings LLC in writing and identifying the requesting
authority, the legal basis, and the specific account(s) or record(s) sought.
Informal or verbal requests are declined and directed to this process.

## 3. Review of every request

The Founder (with legal counsel where warranted) reviews each request before any
data moves. Review confirms: (a) the process is legally valid and properly served;
(b) the requesting authority has jurisdiction; (c) the scope is specific and no
broader than the law compels. JSH rejects, or seeks to narrow through available
legal channels, any request that is invalid, overbroad, vague, or that seeks data
JSH does not hold. Where a request is unenforceable against JSH, JSH does not
comply voluntarily.

## 4. Minimum necessary disclosure

When disclosure is legally compelled, JSH produces only the specific data elements
the process names, for only the accounts named, for only the time period named.
JSH does not volunteer additional data, categories, or accounts.

## 5. User notification

JSH notifies the affected user of a government request for their data before
disclosure where legally permitted, giving them an opportunity to seek protective
relief. Notice may be delayed or withheld only where a legally binding
non-disclosure order or statute prohibits it, or where notice creates a credible
risk of harm to a person; where notice is delayed, JSH provides it once the
prohibition lapses.

## 6. Processors

Requests received by JSH's processors (Supabase Inc., Netlify Inc.) concerning
JSH-controlled data are to be redirected to JSH as controller per the processing
terms; JSH then applies this policy.

## 7. Emergency requests

In a genuine emergency involving imminent risk of death or serious physical injury,
JSH may disclose the minimum data necessary to address the emergency, documented
and reviewed by the Founder, consistent with applicable law.

## 8. Records

JSH keeps a record of every government data request received, its review outcome,
what (if anything) was disclosed, and user-notice status.
